The Fuel Lag

Python

A time-series analysis of UK CPI data tracing how diesel price shocks move through supply chains into food inflation. Bread, fish and vegetables are hit hardest.

For Lidl: a way to think about how cost shocks can travel through food supply chains, giving supplier teams another signal to consider when monitoring cost pressures and continuity.

More Inputs, Less Profit

Cost-benefit analysis of organic and integrated smallholder production of amaranth and corchorus. Organic production returned 19.9% ROI versus −18.4% for integrated production and remained more profitable across price and cost scenarios.

For Lidl: a structured way to assess whether a production practice is economically viable alongside its sustainability benefits, an important consideration when working with suppliers on changes to farming practices.

MSc Dissertation: Resilience in UK Surplus Food Redistribution

NVivo

A qualitative document analysis of reports from UK food redistribution organisations and government, exploring the pressures the sector faced during the Russia–Ukraine war and how organisations responded.

For Lidl: a grounded understanding of what happens to surplus food after it leaves the store and where redistribution partners come under strain, relevant to waste reduction and the sustainability side of the programme. I also volunteer with The Felix Project, so I've seen this work first-hand.
